## Project Summary

Development of a modern, high-performance custom e-commerce website for TechTrendIndia, replacing the existing WordPress/WooCommerce setup. The new store will be faster, mobile-optimized, SEO-friendly, and require zero monthly hosting/plugin costs.

**Current issues with WordPress site:**
- Slow loading speed (4–8 seconds)
- Monthly plugin renewal costs (₹5,000–12,000/year)
- Hosting fees (₹3,000–8,000/year)
- Frequent downtime after WordPress/plugin updates
- Poor mobile experience
- Dependent on multiple third-party plugins for basic functionality

**What the new site delivers:**
- Page load time under 1 second
- Zero hosting cost (deployed on Vercel free tier)
- Zero plugin dependency (everything custom-built)
- Free SSL certificate included
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Built-in SEO (sitemap, meta tags, structured data)
- Razorpay payment integration (UPI, cards, net banking, COD)

## Scope of Work

### ✅ Included in this project

| # | Deliverable | Details |
|---|------------|---------|
| 1 | **Home page** | Hero banner (rotating), category grid, flash sale section with countdown timer, new arrivals, featured products carousel, brand showcase, trust badges, newsletter signup |
| 2 | **Product listing pages** | Filter by category, price range, discount, stock availability. Sort by price, newest, discount. Pagination. |

| 8 | **Order confirmation page** | Order number, delivery timeline, item list, payment summary |
| 9 | **Search** | Instant typeahead search with product suggestions, category suggestions, search results page with filters |
| 10 | **Wishlist** | Save products for later, move to cart |
| 11 | **Static pages** | About us, Contact (with form), FAQ, Shipping & returns policy, Privacy policy, Terms of service |
| 12 | **SEO setup** | XML sitemap, robots.txt, Open Graph meta tags, product structured data (Google rich results), proper alt text on all images |
| 13 | **Mobile responsive** | Full mobile optimization with app-style bottom navigation, touch-friendly interactions |
| 14 | **Deployment** | Live on Vercel, connected to your domain (techtrendindia.com), SSL configured |
